Glowkept support

Glowkept organizes the dates and places in your photos into a personal map, timeline, and memory constellation. Requires iOS 17 / iPadOS 17 or later.

Contact: minimoss@126.com. Include the app version, iOS version, device model, steps to reproduce, and only the redacted screenshots needed to explain the issue. Please do not send your full photo library or unnecessary private information.

  • Getting started: Explore the sample on the welcome screen, or connect Apple Photos and authorize the photos you want to use.
  • Missing photos on the map: Check access permissions, valid dates and locations, and active filters. Settings includes photo management and manual completion tools.
  • Language: Choose a preferred system language or per-app language in iOS Settings. Supported versions are English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Japanese, Korean, German, French, Spanish for Spain, Spanish for Latin America, Brazilian Portuguese, and Italian. English is the fallback.
  • Originals: Removing photos from the app or clearing app data does not delete originals in Apple Photos; it can remove local organization information.
  • Network use: Maps and place lookups may need the network. iCloud originals may need a system download.
  • Keeping your organization: Export organization data from Settings and store it safely. Use Apple Photos and your own backup arrangements for originals.

In the app, go to Settings and open the Storage and privacy section to find the privacy policy and support information.
