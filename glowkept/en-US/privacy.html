Glowkept privacy policy

Glowkept is operated by Minimoss Studio. For privacy questions or technical support, email minimoss@126.com.

This policy takes effect on the date Glowkept is first released to the public on the App Store.

Photos and organization data

Glowkept accesses the photos you authorize and their dates, locations, resource identifiers, and necessary image content to organize your map, timeline, constellation, and experiences on your device. Originals remain managed by Apple Photos. The app does not upload originals to a developer-run photo cloud, or modify, move, or delete originals in Apple Photos.

Titles, notes, manual dates and places, selections, chapters, and saved items are stored in the app locally. Limited photo access restricts the app to resources you select. You can change or revoke photo access in iOS Settings. Revocation can make photos unavailable; separately saved organization text may remain until you clear app data. When you choose to save a chapter as a system album, the app updates that album’s name and photo membership after confirmation, without rewriting original photo files. Album changes may sync according to your iCloud Photos settings.

Location, maps, and network services

The app requests when-in-use device location only after you tap the current-location button. It does not request continuous background location. Browsing locations already recorded in photos does not require device-location permission.

Apple system services provide maps, place searches, and reverse geocoding. These services may send coordinates, search terms, and information needed to provide the service to Apple, subject to Apple's applicable policies, including Maps and Privacy.

If originals are stored only in iCloud, the system photo service may download them according to your Apple settings. Uncached maps and originals not on the device may not be available offline.

Accounts, advertising, and analytics

The current app requires no account and includes no developer-run account service, advertising SDK, or behavioral analytics SDK. The developer does not use this app to track your activity across other apps for advertising. Material changes to these practices require updates to this policy and store privacy information.

Sharing, exports, and system backups

You initiate sharing and exports and choose the recipient. Shared items may include photos, text, dates, or places. Review the content before sending it. Recipients and external apps process received information under their own rules.

Organization exports are not backups of original photos. User-created organization data participates in system backup, while the rebuildable index is excluded. Actual backup and iCloud Photos behavior depends on your system settings and Apple services. The app is not an original-photo backup service.

Retention, deletion, and security

Local organization data remains until you change it, clear app data, or uninstall the app. Restoration after reinstallation depends on system backups. Manage backup copies separately through Apple's backup settings. Clearing app data deletes the app's index, notes, manual places, and organization relationships; it does not delete originals in Apple Photos. Export organization data you want to keep before clearing or uninstalling.

The app relies on the iOS sandbox and system file protection. Protect access to your device and exported files. Security measures cannot eliminate every risk.

Contact and updates

Email minimoss@126.com with questions, to report an issue, or to request deletion of the support emails and attachments you sent us. We use information you choose to send only to handle your request. Please do not send your full photo library.

We keep support emails and attachments for up to 90 days after your request is resolved, then delete them. You can email us to request earlier deletion. We retain them longer only when required by law.
